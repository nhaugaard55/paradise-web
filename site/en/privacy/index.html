Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

The Paradise (Hostería Paradise, Segunda Bajada al Mar, Puerto Pirámides, Chubut, Argentina) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Contact form: name, email address and message. These are sent via Formspree and used solely to reply to your enquiry.
  • Analytics cookies (optional): if you accept cookies, Google Tag Manager may collect anonymous browsing data (pages visited, time on site, device) for statistical purposes.

2. How we use your data

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To analyse site traffic and improve the browsing experience (only if you accepted cookies).

3. Cookies

We use analytics cookies via Google Tag Manager. On your first visit you can accept or reject their use. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except with the service providers mentioned (Formspree for email delivery, Google Analytics if you accepted cookies) and only for the purposes stated above.

5. Data retention

Contact form messages are kept in our inbox for as long as needed to handle your enquiry. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymous.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by writing to paradisepiramides26@gmail.com.

7. Contact

For any questions about this policy, write to paradisepiramides26@gmail.com or visit our Contact section.
